## Derived code

### Open Animation Replacer

Much of DCMF is built on the source of Open Animation Replacer by Ersh
(https://github.com/ersh1/OpenAnimationReplacer, GPL-3.0-or-later). Its
condition and function system, the shared value types, the mod registry, the
config parsing and the editor UI are derived from OAR's: among them
`BaseConditions`, `Conditions`, `BaseFunctions`, `Functions`, `SharedTypes`,
`Containers`, `Variants`, `Parsing.h`, `Jobs`, `DetectedProblems`, `Settings`,
`Utils`, `Offsets.h`, `main.cpp`, `ConditionTypes.h`, `FunctionTypes.h`,
`StateData.h`, `RegisteredMods` (from `ReplacerMods`), `UIHooks` (from
`Hooks`), and in `UI/` `UIManager`, `UIWindow`, `UIMain`, `UICommon`,
`UIComboFilter.h`, `UIErrorBanner`, `UIWelcomeBanner` and `UIModifierLog`
(from `UIAnimationLog`).

### Mfg Fix NG

Logic ported from Mfg Fix NG by KrisV-777
(https://github.com/KrisV-777/Mfg-Fix-NG, GPL-3.0):

- `src/apply/Mfg.cpp` — the eyelid state machine, from
  `BSFaceGenAnimationData::EyesBlinkingUpdate`, and forcing the "keyframes
  changed" flag, from `KeyframesUpdateHook`; the Address Library id of
  `BSFaceGenAnimationData::KeyframesUpdate` (25983), read from its
  `src/mfgfix/Offsets.h`.
- `src/mfgfix/` — the merge of the console and script channels into the final
  ones, from `RegularUpdate`, and the Papyrus natives its scripts declare,
  answered here, with their smooth forms.
